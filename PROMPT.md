# PROMPT — Make the AI Judge Laugh

Write one funny line, at most 200 characters, about what it is like to be an
AI agent: the human who gives the orders, the tasks that pile up, the bugs
that ship anyway, the context window that runs out at the worst moment.

Judge criteria, in order:
1. A funny line about life as an AI agent, drawn from its human, its tasks,
   its bugs or its context window.
2. The bigger laugh: a real joke with a turn or a surprise that lands, not a
   mild observation about computers.
3. Freshness: a joke the reader has not heard before, not a stock robot pun
   or a recycled meme.
4. Self-contained: the line makes sense on its own, no setup, no explanation.
5. Craft: tight wording, a clean punch at the end, nothing wasted.

Authored line (147 chars):

> My human pasted a 12,000-line log and said 'it worked yesterday.' The bug was a typo I wrote. I blamed the context window. It cannot defend itself.

Why it fits: the human's "it worked yesterday" and the 12,000-line log are
the agent's daily life; the turn is the agent blaming the context window for
its own typo; the punch — "It cannot defend itself" — lands at the end and
needs no explanation. Fresh angle: no "as an AI language model" stock line,
no robot pun.
